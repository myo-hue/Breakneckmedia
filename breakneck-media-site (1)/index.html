About

Myo — founder

From a young age, I've been obsessed with filming and editing my surroundings — always looking for ways to turn everyday moments into stories. Photography and videography aren't a side interest, they're how I see things.

I started Break Neck Media to build that into something real: a place to grow my experience in creative media and content creation, one shoot at a time. Flying drones lets me take it further — capturing angles and perspectives that don't exist from the ground.

Myo, Founder
